Strategic Logic · Why Here · Why Now

The GCC is Where Sovereign Technology Gets Built.

Bahrain and Dubai are not chosen for optics. They are chosen because the functional separation of an R&D engine and a commercial gateway is only achievable in a jurisdiction where government support, industrial land, and investor access occupy the same geography.

01

Bahrain: Where Technology Gets Made

Lower-cost industrial base. Government-supported BIIP zone. 50% electricity rebate years 1-5. Large-format land for R&D labs, outdoor test yards, academy, compute, and H2 power systems. Bahrain carries the build. Dubai stays lean.

02

Dubai: Where Deals Get Done

Jafza free zone gives procurement tax efficiency, investor showroom access, and GCC commercial credibility. Sovereign funds, family offices and enterprise buyers fly into Dubai. Dubai is the front door.

03

IP: Protected and Proprietary

Imagitech holds all intellectual property — Octanet mmWave, electromagnetic generation, proprietary ASIC and mining chip design, and the AI/compute platform. All technologies are designed, developed and owned exclusively by Imagitech. No third-party licensing.

04

GCC Alignment with National Vision

Bahrain Vision 2030, Saudi Vision 2030, UAE AI Strategy 2031 — all three explicitly call for sovereign AI infrastructure, technology independence and national human capital development. The Imagitech platform is purpose-built to deliver against all three.

05

Projects Already in Motion

Live deployments in the Philippines, an active project in Langdon Hills UK, an active programme in Brazil, and the Australia SDEIP pilot. The Bahrain hub services all of them — prototype validation, remote NOC, trained deployment crews, and supply chain coordination.

06

One Platform. Not Two Sites.

Bahrain builds and operates. Dubai shows, sells and scales. Together they create a cost-to-visibility ratio no single-site strategy achieves inside the same $20M envelope — and two live GCC strategic footholds that improve investor confidence for every future raise.
